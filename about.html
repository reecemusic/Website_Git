About Reece

Established 1991 in an English seaside town, I've been fortunate enough to travel some of the world pursuing a enjoyment for engineering whilst managing to haul a piano with me.

I began learning the keyboard, and then piano. Having always had a curiosity for stringed instruments I thought I'd try to keep it simple and started with the Ukulele, eventually falling in love with that instrument too.

I currently find myself in the middle east and shall be releasing demo music soon.
